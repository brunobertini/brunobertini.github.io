I am a Royal Society University Research Fellow and Associate Professor at the University of Birmingham. 



My research aims to build a systematic understanding of quantum matter in highly excited non-equilibrium states. My approach is to create original understanding extracting the essence from exact solutions of minimal interacting models. My work is mainly analytical, combining ideas from statistical mechanics, quantum information, quantum integrability, quantum chaos, and random matrix theory. For more information about my research interests, my publications, and my group please follow the links above.
